Large language models (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude) have problems answering regional questions. Sometimes, they only know the answer in the local language and/or English and fail to answer in other languages, and sometimes the answers radically differ across languages. The goal of the project is to create a challenge set that will test the abilities to answer region-specific questions across languages.

Your task is to browse Wikipedia and find facts that are very specific to your country (Czech Republic, Slovakia, Ukraine). Select the facts that you think many people in that country know but almost everyone outside the country does not.

Detailed instructions

  • The “Skip button” takes you a random page from our automatically preselected list. You do not have to rely on this: Click the links in the Wikipedia articles to get to more relevant articles.
  • Use each Wikipedia page just once.
  • Only consider Wikipedia pages about local entities (E.g., a Czech page providing unique details about a Croatian village is not what we want.)
  • Please avoid facts that you think almost no one knows or consider rather obscure.
  • Write a question and answer in Czech and provide an English translation of both.
  • If the Wikipedia page has a photograph (not a computer-drawn scheme) that illustrates the topic and allows you to ask a reasonable question about it, click it. The URL of the image should show up in the form.
  • Formulate a question and answer related to the image. It can be a small reformulation of the previous question or a new one.
